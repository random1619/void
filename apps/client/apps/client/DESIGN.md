# Design Direction — Ivory Atelier

## Aesthetic World
**Warm editorial ivory** — A cohesive design system built around:
- **Palette:** Ivory/cream base (`#faf8f5`), deep ink text (`#1a1814`), burnt sienna accent (`#b4552d`)
- **Typography:** Playfair Display for headlines (oversized, high-contrast serifs), system sans-serif for body
- **Texture:** Hairline rules (1px), subtle gradients, translucent overlays
- **Density:** Generous whitespace, breathing room around key elements
- **Personality:** Restrained luxury, editorial sophistication, craftsmanship-forward

## Primary Aesthetic Family
**Editorial Fashion** — Inspired by high-end fashion magazines and luxury brand lookbooks. Think Vogue, The Row, Hermès editorial presentations.

## Accent
**One burnt-sienna accent color** per composition — used sparingly for:
- Index numbers (01, 02, 03)
- Hover states on links
- Section eyebrows
- Decorative lines and frames

## Coherence Rules
1. **Restraint over novelty** — One world executed consistently beats disconnected experiments
2. **Typography hierarchy** — Display type is oversized and bold, body is comfortable and readable
3. **Motion personality** — Smooth, luxury easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), never bouncy or playful
4. **Spatial consistency** — Elements enter/exit along consistent paths, anchored to their triggers

## Current State (as of initial review)
The landing page follows the ivory editorial direction with:
- Hero section with staggered word animation
- Editorial lookbook images with hairline frame accents
- Stats section with numbered indexes
- Category showcase, featured products, testimonials
- Newsletter CTA

## Design Principles
- **Quality over quantity** — Show fewer, better images
- **Hierarchy through scale** — Large display type, comfortable body text
- **Purposeful motion** — Animations convey spatial relationships, not decoration
- **Accessibility-first** — Contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML
